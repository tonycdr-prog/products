# ChaseWell UK

## What it does
ChaseWell UK generates a practical late-invoice chase pack for UK B2B freelancers and SMEs. The static MVP now calculates overdue days from the due date, estimates statutory interest using editable base-rate-plus-8% logic, applies the fixed compensation band, creates dated escalation steps, generates email/final-notice/payment-summary copy, and stores local case history for follow-up.

## Target customer
UK freelancers, consultants, agencies and small businesses that invoice other businesses and need a calm, repeatable way to chase overdue payments without sounding aggressive or inventing legal wording from scratch.

## Pricing model
- Free: 3 chase packs/month, local-only output.
- Founder plan: £24/month for unlimited packs, branded templates, CSV upload and payment-link tracking.
- Agency: £79/month for multi-client history, team templates and reporting.

## Competitive advantage
A focused UK-first cashflow wedge: not a generic invoicing suite, not a debt-collection agency. It combines polite copy, payment math, evidence readiness and escalation timing in one browser flow, with privacy-friendly local processing for the MVP.

## MVP boundary
Self-contained static HTML/CSS/JS. No backend, accounts, payment collection, email sending or legal review workflow yet. It is a drafting aid, not legal advice.
