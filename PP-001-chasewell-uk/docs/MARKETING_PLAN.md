# Marketing plan — ChaseWell UK

## Landing page headline
Chase overdue invoices with numbers, dates and nerve.

## Three bullet benefits
- Generate a complete UK B2B chase pack in under two minutes.
- Estimate statutory interest, fixed compensation and total claim value before you send.
- Keep a local history of late invoices and export follow-up data as CSV.

## CTA copy
Build my chase pack → Join the £24/month founder plan.

## Primary distribution channel
Founder-led LinkedIn and accountant/bookkeeper partnerships: post practical examples of overdue-invoice scripts, then offer 10 free chase-pack reviews for UK freelancers and small agencies.

## First-week launch tactics
- Add Tally or ConvertKit capture to replace local waitlist storage.
- Add Stripe Payment Link for the founder plan.
- Publish a short walkthrough GIF/video showing invoice entry → copied reminder → saved case history.
- DM accountants/bookkeepers with a free template they can share with clients.
- Track: visits, generated packs, copied reminders, saved cases, waitlist signups and paid founder-plan clicks.
