# TaxGap Finder

## What it does
Stop missing sole-trader deductions. The MVP is a self-contained browser app with real calculations/generation and no backend dependency.

## Who it's for
UK sole traders, freelancers, tradespeople.

## Pricing strategy
£49/year or £10/month during tax season. Start with waitlist/manual fulfillment, then add Stripe checkout and account storage only after demand.
