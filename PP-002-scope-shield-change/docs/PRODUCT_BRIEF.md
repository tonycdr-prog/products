# ChangeOrder Shield Product Brief

## What it does
ChangeOrder Shield turns scope creep into a priced, client-ready change order. A freelancer or small agency enters the original scope, the new client request, extra hours, rate, complexity, rush premium, VAT setting, payment terms, and approval deadline. The app generates:

- Suggested fee with margin buffer
- Timeline impact
- Risk level
- Formal change order
- Client approval email
- Phone-call script
- Approval-readiness checklist
- Local saved change orders

## Target customer
Freelancers, consultants, and small agencies who routinely absorb "quick extra" client requests because pricing and wording a change order feels awkward.

## Pricing model
- Free static generator for acquisition and trust.
- £29 Change Order Pack: PDF export, six reusable templates, approval tracker, and clause library.
- £79/month Agency Pro: team templates, branded approvals, client history, and analytics.

## Competitive advantage
Most proposal tools help win new work. ChangeOrder Shield focuses on protecting margin inside active projects, at the exact moment a client asks for unpaid extra work. It is fast, practical, and positioned as a revenue-protection tool rather than generic document generation.

## Current MVP status
The static browser MVP is usable now without accounts, build step, or network access. It calculates fee/timeline/risk, creates three reusable outputs, supports clipboard copy, and stores recent change orders in localStorage.

## Production next step
Wire the £29 CTA to Stripe Payment Links, add a PDF/export flow, and collect email/payment conversion metrics from targeted freelancer/agency distribution.
