# Marketing Plan

## Landing page headline
Stop doing "one small extra" for free.

## Three bullet benefits
- Price scope creep in under two minutes with fee, VAT, risk, and timeline impact.
- Send a polished change order and approval email without sounding confrontational.
- Protect project margin with a repeatable checklist before work starts.

## CTA copy
Primary: Generate my change order
Paid: Buy the £29 Change Order Pack

## One distribution channel
Freelancer and agency communities where people discuss scope creep: post a practical "scope creep response script" and link to the free generator only after providing the template in the post.

## First launch sequence
1. Deploy static MVP with analytics and a Stripe Payment Link placeholder converted to live payment.
2. Share a useful script/checklist post: "How to say yes to extra work without doing it free."
3. DM or reply to 10 freelancers/agencies already discussing scope creep and ask them to test one real client request.
4. Add the top three objections to the page: fear of annoying clients, uncertainty around pricing, and whether VAT/payment terms should be included.
5. Test the £29 Change Order Pack with PDF export and six common templates.
