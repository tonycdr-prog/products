# Marketing plan — BillArchaeology

## Landing page headline

Find the clause that quietly leaks SaaS revenue.

## Three benefits

- Turn vague billing clauses into concrete invoice checks in under two minutes.
- Spot underbilling, discount, overage, minimum-commitment, and renewal ambiguity before the next billing run.
- Package one ugly clause into a £49 manual audit request for fast validation.

## CTA copy

- Primary: Run the free billing archaeology check
- Secondary: Package a £49 manual audit
- Lead capture: Send me the contract billing audit checklist

## Distribution channel

Direct LinkedIn/email outreach to 10 RevOps consultants and fractional CFOs. Ask for one anonymised ugly billing clause, run it through the checker, and offer the £49 manual audit for the worst cases.

## First-week validation steps

1. Add the real Stripe Payment Link to the local setting in the MVP.
2. Send 10 highly specific asks: “Got a clause where discounts/overages/renewals keep causing invoice disputes?”
3. Publish one anonymised teardown post showing a clause clarity score and variance calculation.
4. Track replies, saved leads, and manual-audit purchases in a simple sheet.
5. If 2+ people pay or request review, build backend lead capture and a shared audit workspace.
