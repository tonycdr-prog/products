# DepositProof UK Product Brief

## What it does
Build a TDS-ready evidence response in 12 minutes.

## Who it is for
UK renters, tenant advisers, and letting-agency complaint handlers.

## Problem
Tenants need to challenge deductions, suspicious invoices, and weak check-out evidence quickly.

## MVP
Single-page browser tool with structured inputs, generated action pack, .txt export, waitlist, pricing CTA, and no backend.

## Pricing strategy
£9 one-off pack / £15/mo for repeat users. Start with concierge review upsell only after validation.
