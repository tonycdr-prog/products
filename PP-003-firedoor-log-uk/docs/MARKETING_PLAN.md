# Marketing plan — FireDoor Log UK

## Distribution channels
1. Reddit value posts in the exact communities surfaced during research.
2. LinkedIn posts aimed at UK SMEs/property managers/freelancers.
3. SEO landing page targeting long-tail pain queries.
4. Direct outreach to niche accountants/managing agents/FM consultants.
5. Founder-led cold email with one useful free checklist.

## First-week launch tactics
- Publish a transparent “I built this after seeing X pain” post, not a spam pitch.
- Offer 10 free manual reviews in exchange for feedback.
- Add Tally/ConvertKit capture and Stripe Payment Link.
- Ship one mini case study from the first useful generated output.
- Track replies, signups, paid clicks, and refund objections.
