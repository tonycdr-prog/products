# FireDoor Log UK

## What it does
Fire-door inspection log and evidence-pack generator for monthly checks and remedial close-out.

## Who it is for
Resident directors, small block managers, FM consultants.

## Pricing strategy
£39/mo single block; £99/mo portfolio; £199/mo consultant. Keep founder offer simple, validate willingness to pay before building accounts/auth.

## MVP boundary
Static HTML/CSS/JS, local-only waitlist capture, no backend or payments wired yet.
