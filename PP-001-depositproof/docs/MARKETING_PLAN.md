# DepositProof marketing plan

## Landing page headline
Turn a messy deposit deduction into a scheme-ready rebuttal pack.

## Three bullet benefits
- Spot missing agent evidence before you respond.
- Generate a calm DPS/TDS/mydeposits-ready dispute draft in minutes.
- Export a labelled evidence pack you can paste, print, or send.

## CTA copy
Primary: Build my free rebuttal pack
Paid: Get the full £15 evidence pack
Upsell: Request £49 manual review

## One distribution channel
Reddit HousingUK validation replies/posts: respond to live deposit-deduction threads with practical advice, disclose the builder, and invite users to try the free pack. Track pack generations, lead captures, and paid CTA clicks from those 10 touches.

## First-week launch tactics
1. Deploy the static page and add analytics around `Generate rebuttal pack`, paid CTA, and checkout opens.
2. Create Stripe Payment Links for £15 Full Pack and £49 Manual Review; paste the £15 link into the page’s Stripe field for validation.
3. Do 10 targeted validation touches in relevant UK tenant/deposit discussions.
4. Turn the generated pack into a short example walkthrough for SEO: “How to dispute an inflated cleaning deduction”.
5. Kill or reposition if there are 100 targeted visits with zero pack generations and zero leads.

## Success criteria
- 25%+ of targeted visitors generate a pack.
- 5%+ enter email or click paid CTA.
- At least one paid checkout or manual-review request within the first 10 serious validation touches.
