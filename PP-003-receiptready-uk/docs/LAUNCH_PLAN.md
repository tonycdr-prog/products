# ReceiptReady UK — Launch Plan

## Week 1 Distribution

### Day 1 — Show HN
Post to Hacker News as a simple, working browser tool:

- Venue: https://news.ycombinator.com/show
- Angle: “local-first tax admin helper, no uploads, vanilla HTML/JS”
- Goal: get technical feedback on trust, privacy and whether the calculator feels safe enough to use.

### Day 2 — Indie Hackers
Post in the Product/Build in Public audience:

- Venue: https://www.indiehackers.com/
- Angle: narrow paid wedge for UK freelancers: “can a tiny tool beat a spreadsheet before Self Assessment?”
- Goal: find founders/freelancers who would pay £9/month for saved packs and recurring templates.

### Day 3 — Reddit
Post only where rules allow useful tools and avoid spammy framing.

- r/UKPersonalFinance — only if framed as a free preparation tool and with “not tax advice” caveat.
- r/FreelanceUK — best audience for messy receipt pain.
- r/smallbusinessuk — ask owners/bookkeepers if the checklist matches their workflow.
- r/Bookkeeping — ask bookkeepers if the handoff summary reduces client chasing.

### Day 4–5 — LinkedIn/Founder Posts
Share a short before/after screenshot thread targeting UK sole traders and bookkeepers. Ask for 5 people to test with last year’s receipts.

### Day 6–7 — Follow-up
Collect objections, then update copy around the top 3 points: tax confidence, privacy/no uploads, and accountant handoff usefulness.

## First Post Template

Title: I built a free local-first receipt pack builder for UK freelancers

Body:

I kept seeing the same filing-season problem: freelancers have receipts, mileage notes and bank transactions, but not a clean handoff for their accountant/bookkeeper.

So I built ReceiptReady UK — a free browser-only tool that helps you:

- add expenses and business mileage,
- flag missing evidence or missing business purpose notes,
- estimate an allowable claim,
- export a CSV and accountant checklist.

It runs locally in the browser: no uploads, no account, no external dependencies. It is preparation support, not tax advice.

I’m testing whether the paid version should be £9/month for saved packs, recurring expense templates and better accountant handoff exports.

Would this replace your receipt spreadsheet, or is there a missing step that would stop you using it?

Link: https://tonycdr-prog.github.io/products/PP-003-receiptready-uk/

## Cold Outreach Targets

1. **UK bookkeeping practice owner — LinkedIn**
   - Search terms: “UK bookkeeper Self Assessment clients”, “sole trader bookkeeper”, “receipt chasing”.
   - Ask: would a free receipt-pack checklist reduce client back-and-forth?

2. **Freelancer/accountant content creator — LinkedIn or X**
   - Search terms: “UK freelancer tax tips”, “Self Assessment sole trader expenses”.
   - Ask: can they test the tool and share what would make it credible enough to recommend?

3. **Operations/admin VA serving freelancers — LinkedIn**
   - Search terms: “virtual assistant UK freelancers bookkeeping admin”, “business admin support sole traders”.
   - Ask: do clients already pay them to organise receipts, and would a structured export save time?

## Success Criteria

- 10 meaningful visits from targeted posts.
- 3 replies from bookkeepers/freelancers naming a missing feature or workflow blocker.
- 2 waitlist emails or direct replies interested in saved packs/templates.
- At least 1 bookkeeper willing to review a Pro accountant handoff export.
