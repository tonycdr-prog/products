# Marketing Plan — ReceiptReady UK

## Landing page headline
Turn a year of receipts into a clean HMRC-ready expense pack.

## 3 bullet benefits
- Spot missing receipt evidence before your accountant has to chase you.
- Estimate allowable claims, mileage allowance and basic-rate tax saving in minutes.
- Export a clean CSV + checklist from a private browser-only workflow.

## CTA copy
Build my free receipt pack

## Distribution channel
Start with direct outreach to UK bookkeepers/freelance accountants: offer the free tool as a client-prep checklist and ask for 5–10 messy real-world examples to validate the paid handoff/export features.

## First-week launch moves
1. Publish the static tool and add a Tally waitlist + Stripe payment link for the £39 clean-up pack.
2. DM 20 UK bookkeepers with a concise “can this reduce client receipt chasing?” ask.
3. Post a practical “Self Assessment receipt pack checklist” on LinkedIn and relevant freelancer communities.
4. Convert repeated objections into product copy and the next feature list.
