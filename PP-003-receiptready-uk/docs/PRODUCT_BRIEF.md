# ReceiptReady UK

## What it does
ReceiptReady UK is a browser-only expense pack builder for UK freelancers and sole traders. Users add expenses, mileage, receipt status, business-use percentage and purpose notes; the app calculates an allowable claim estimate, flags evidence gaps, produces an accountant checklist, and exports a CSV.

## Target customer
UK sole traders, freelancers, side-hustlers and bookkeepers handling messy receipt evidence before Self Assessment or quarterly bookkeeping reviews.

## Pricing model
- Free public tool: one local expense pack + CSV export.
- Starter: £15/month for saved packs, templates, recurring expenses and accountant handoff exports.
- Tax-year clean-up pack: £39 one-off for guided checklist/export bundle during filing season.

## Competitive advantage
Local-first, no-account workflow makes it safe to test with sensitive financial admin. The wedge is narrower than generic bookkeeping tools: it turns receipt mess into a shareable evidence pack rather than trying to replace accounting software.

## Current MVP status
Self-contained `index.html` with no external CDN dependencies. Working features: multi-expense ledger, mileage allowance estimate, evidence risk scoring, local save/restore, CSV export, printable summary, checklist, and local waitlist capture stub.
