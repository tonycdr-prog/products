# ReceiptReady UK

## Refined Target Customer
UK freelancers, sole traders and small bookkeepers who hit the same filing-season problem: receipts are scattered, bank transactions lack business context, mileage is under-documented, and the accountant handoff turns into an expensive back-and-forth.

The tightest early adopter is a UK sole trader earning enough to care about allowable expenses but not enough to justify full bookkeeping software: designers, consultants, trades-adjacent independents, creators, coaches and side-hustlers preparing Self Assessment or quarterly reviews.

## Promise
Turn a messy pile of receipts, mileage notes and partial evidence into a clean accountant-ready expense pack in under 15 minutes.

## What It Does Today
ReceiptReady UK is a self-contained browser tool. Users can:

- Add expense lines with date, supplier, category, amount, receipt status, business-use percentage and purpose notes.
- Add business mileage and estimate allowance at 45p/mile.
- See an allowable claim estimate, basic-rate tax saving estimate, evidence-gap count and pack readiness score.
- Generate an accountant checklist and printable pack summary.
- Export a CSV for accountant/bookkeeper review.
- Join a waitlist for Pro; the demo stores the lead locally and opens a mailto draft.

No external dependencies, no uploads, no account required for the free tool.

## Pricing Model
- **Free:** one local expense pack, evidence risk score, CSV export and printable summary.
- **Pro — £9/month:** saved packs across tax years, recurring expense templates, accountant handoff exports and evidence prompts.

The £9/month plan fits the product better than a one-off because receipt capture and bookkeeping reviews recur throughout the tax year. A filing-season annual discount can be tested later.

## Revenue Proximity
High. The core utility is already usable without backend infrastructure. The next paid step is not complex accounting automation; it is saved packs, repeat templates and a cleaner accountant handoff format.

## Distribution Tactic
Partner-led micro-distribution through UK bookkeepers and accountant-adjacent creators:

1. Post the free tool in UK freelancer/accounting communities with a specific ask: “does this CSV/checklist reduce your receipt-chasing?”
2. DM small UK bookkeepers on LinkedIn who post Self Assessment content and offer a co-branded “receipt pack checklist” for their clients.
3. Turn the best feedback into a comparison page: “ReceiptReady vs spreadsheet receipt tracker for UK sole traders.”

## Compliance Notes
ReceiptReady should stay clear that it is preparation support, not tax advice. Use wording like “allowable claim estimate” and “confirm with HMRC guidance or a qualified accountant.”
