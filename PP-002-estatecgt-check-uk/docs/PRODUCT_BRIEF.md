# Product Brief — EstateCGT Check UK

A preparation tool for UK executors selling inherited property. It estimates gain, per-share exposure, illustrative CGT and 60-day reporting deadline, then generates questions for a solicitor/accountant.

## Audience
UK executors, beneficiaries and probate support firms.

## Pricing
Free browser check; £15 one-off Executor Pack; £79/mo white-label probate-firm pack. Conservative target: 30 packs/month = £450 MRR-equivalent.
