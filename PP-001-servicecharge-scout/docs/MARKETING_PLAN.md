# Marketing plan

## Landing headline
Spot questionable UK leasehold service charges before you pay for advice.

## Three benefits
- Find charge lines with unusual year-on-year increases in under 10 minutes.
- Generate a document checklist and managing-agent email from your own figures.
- Turn messy demands into a print-ready triage pack for resident-group review.

## CTA copy
Run the free checker — upgrade to the £29 triage pack when you need the printable report.

## Distribution channel
Start with helpful, non-spammy answers in UK leasehold / HousingUK / resident association discussions: share the free checker plus a short checklist article, then invite affected users to the £29 paid pack.

## First-week plan
1. Deploy the static page and add analytics-free event logging or a simple waitlist form.
2. Add Stripe Payment Link/Gumroad checkout for the £29 pack.
3. Recruit 10 leaseholders/resident directors for sample reports.
4. Publish one article: “How to challenge vague service-charge increases without wasting solicitor time.”
5. Collect anonymised before/after charge-line examples for benchmark copy.
