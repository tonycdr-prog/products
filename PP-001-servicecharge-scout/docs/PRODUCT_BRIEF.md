# ServiceCharge Scout UK — Product brief

## What it does
ServiceCharge Scout UK is a browser-based triage tool for UK leaseholders reviewing annual service-charge demands or accounts. Users enter current and previous charge lines, note available evidence, and receive:

- anomaly/risk score based on year-on-year increases and missing evidence
- per-flat exposure estimate
- priority charge-line flags
- document/evidence checklist
- managing-agent email draft
- print/PDF-ready triage report
- local save/load for a single case

## Target customer
Primary: UK leaseholders who suspect vague, inflated, or poorly explained service charges.
Secondary: resident management company directors, resident associations, and small block groups who need structure before paying a solicitor.

## Pricing model
- Free: browser checker and email draft.
- £29: downloadable triage pack with polished PDF, evidence table, and challenge scripts.
- £59/month: resident-group workspace for one block, shared evidence tracker, and repeat-year comparisons.

## Competitive advantage
Focused on the pre-solicitor moment: fast, plain-English, UK-specific triage that helps users ask better questions and organise evidence before escalation. Tonight's iteration moves it from a simple calculator to a usable report generator with line-item analysis and copy-ready correspondence.
