# LeaseLens UK

## What it does
Interactive triage/checklist for service charge reasonableness, documents, consultation and next questions.

## Who it is for
Leaseholders with high annual service charges or Section 20/major works notices.

## Pricing strategy
£9 one-off triage; £29 report+letter pack; £99 residents group. Keep founder offer simple, validate willingness to pay before building accounts/auth.

## MVP boundary
Static HTML/CSS/JS, local-only waitlist capture, no backend or payments wired yet.
