# TrustPacket marketing plan

## Landing page headline
Turn “Do you have SOC2?” into a credible trust packet before the £20k audit.

## Three benefits
- Show prospects real security progress without over-claiming certification.
- Know exactly which controls and evidence are missing before procurement stalls.
- Reuse generated questionnaire answers, gap plans, and customer email copy.

## CTA copy
Build my free trust packet

## Primary distribution channel
Founder-led posts and replies in Indie Hackers/Hacker News/LinkedIn discussions where early B2B SaaS founders complain about enterprise security questionnaires or SOC2 pressure.

## First campaign
Publish a transparent “We are not SOC2 certified yet — here is the packet we send customers” template post. CTA links to the free checker. Offer the £199 audit-prep sprint to the first 10 founders who paste in a real questionnaire.

## Conversion hypothesis
Founders facing active procurement pressure will pay for speed and confidence before they pay for full compliance infrastructure. The free checker captures the pain moment; the paid offer is stronger exports, evidence vault, and guided review.
