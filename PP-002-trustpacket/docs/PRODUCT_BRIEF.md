# TrustPacket product brief

## What it does
TrustPacket is a self-serve pre-SOC2 trust packet builder for solo SaaS founders. It lets a founder:

- score lightweight SOC2 readiness across nine practical controls
- attach evidence notes or links per control
- generate a customer-facing trust packet
- draft common security questionnaire answers
- produce a gap closure plan and prospect email copy
- save progress locally and download packet text

## Target customer
Solo and tiny-team B2B SaaS founders who are starting to sell into larger customers and keep hearing “Do you have SOC2?” before they can justify a full audit, Vanta/Drata subscription, or security consultant.

## Pricing model
- Free checker: readiness score, gaps, one text export
- Founder workspace: £49/month for saved evidence vault, template policies, PDF exports, customer-room links
- Audit prep sprint: £199 one-off guided review before formal compliance spend

## Competitive advantage
TrustPacket does not pretend to be SOC2 automation. It wins before the compliance tooling purchase by helping founders answer prospects honestly: “not certified yet, but here is our current control evidence and closure plan.” The wedge is speed, plain English, and customer conversation readiness rather than auditor workflow depth.

## Current MVP status
Static browser app. No backend required. Works offline after load. State is stored in localStorage only.
