# TribunalCharge UK Marketing Plan

## Landing page headline
Build a calm, structured service-charge challenge pack before the argument escalates.

## Three bullet benefits
- Spot missing evidence: invoices, Section 20 papers, apportionment schedules and reserve-fund movements.
- Generate a polite but firm managing-agent document request in minutes.
- Save a tribunal-ready chronology, evidence index and escalation checklist without uploading private documents.

## CTA copy
Build my service-charge pack

## Distribution channel
Start with UK leasehold communities where the pain is explicit: Reddit leasehold/renting threads, National Leasehold Campaign-style Facebook groups, and residents’ association forums. Share the free checklist first, then link the tool only where it directly answers a service-charge evidence question.

## First validation sprint
1. Post one useful checklist-style comment in a relevant UK leasehold discussion.
2. DM or email 10 residents’ association organisers asking for feedback on the pack structure.
3. Offer first 10 users a free anonymised pack review in exchange for the exact phrase they searched before finding help.
4. Add a real waitlist/payment link once at least 5 users complete the form or request review.
