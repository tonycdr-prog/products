# TribunalCharge UK Product Brief

## What it does
TribunalCharge UK helps UK leaseholders turn confusing or inflated service-charge demands into a structured evidence pack. The browser-only MVP collects charge figures, lease notes, document gaps, disputed category and timeline events, then generates:

- A service-charge evidence pack with risk level, disputed value and folder index
- A managing-agent/freeholder document request email
- A First-tier Tribunal / escalation readiness checklist
- Local save/load, copy, print and `.txt` export

## Target customer
Primary: UK leaseholders and flat owners facing unclear service-charge increases, major works bills, reserve-fund questions or insurance/management-fee disputes.

Secondary: residents’ associations, leaseholder WhatsApp group organisers, and small leasehold advisers who need a fast intake template before reviewing documents.

## Pricing model
- Free browser triage tool for acquisition
- £15/month early-access tier for unlimited pack generation, examples, and evolving templates
- Later upsell: £49-£99 one-off concierge evidence review or specialist referral fee after demand validation

## Competitive advantage
Most leaseholders start with scattered emails, PDFs and forum posts. TribunalCharge provides a calm, GOV.UK-style structure that converts raw facts into a specific document request, evidence index and escalation checklist without requiring login or uploading private documents.

The key wedge is privacy + speed: the tool is useful immediately, runs fully locally, and produces outputs that are practical enough to send or take to an adviser.

## Current MVP status
Iterated 2026-05-25. The page is self-contained, no external CDN/fonts, and can be opened directly in a browser. It includes document-gap scoring, readiness score, localStorage draft save, export actions and sample data.
