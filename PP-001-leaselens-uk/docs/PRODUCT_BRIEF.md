# LeaseLens UK

## What it does
Charge reasonableness checker + escalation letter generator.

## Who it is for
UK leaseholders, renters, residents associations.

## Pricing strategy
£12/mo individual, £49 residents-association pack. Start with waitlist + £15/mo checkout test; upsell one-off pack/service for urgent cases.

## MVP status
Self-contained HTML/CSS/JS; works locally; no backend; email captured in localStorage for demo only.
