# TrustPacket Product Brief

## What it does
TrustPacket helps early SaaS teams answer vendor security questionnaires without stalling an active deal. The static MVP now generates a buyer-readiness score, identifies missing controls, creates a 7-day remediation plan, drafts a buyer email, and provides copyable questionnaire answers.

## Target customer
Founder-led B2B SaaS companies selling into mid-market or enterprise buyers for the first time, especially teams being asked for SOC 2/ISO evidence, a pentest, or a long security questionnaire before signature.

## Pricing model
- Free: one local browser packet and answer bank.
- Starter: £49/month for saved workspaces, branded trust page, exports, renewal reminders, and customer-specific questionnaire history.
- Setup sprint: £199 one-off done-with-you review for an active sales blocker.

## Competitive advantage
Focused on the painful pre-compliance gap before teams are ready for full GRC software. It produces usable buyer language and remediation priorities immediately, without requiring integrations or security consultants upfront.

## Current MVP scope
Self-contained HTML/CSS/JS. No external CDN dependencies. Works locally with form inputs, localStorage save, readiness scoring, generated trust packet, copy buttons, and text download.

## Next product milestones
1. Persist packets per customer/project.
2. Add branded PDF/HTML trust summary export.
3. Add evidence upload checklist and expiry reminders.
4. Add Stripe checkout for £199 setup sprint.
