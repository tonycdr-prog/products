# TrustPacket Marketing Plan

## Landing page headline
Turn security questionnaires from deal blockers into a buyer-ready trust packet.

## 3 bullet benefits
- Score procurement readiness in minutes across the controls buyers ask about first.
- Generate honest buyer-safe questionnaire answers, a trust summary, and a 7-day remediation plan.
- Avoid premature GRC bloat: fix the sales blocker now, then upgrade when compliance work becomes repeatable.

## CTA copy
Primary: Build my packet  
Secondary: Request a £199 setup sprint

## Distribution channel
Founder/operator outreach in SaaS communities and LinkedIn: post a practical “first enterprise security questionnaire survival checklist,” then offer the free TrustPacket builder to teams with an active questionnaire or pentest blocker.

## First-week launch plan
1. Deploy static page and add analytics.
2. DM 10 B2B SaaS founders/operators who recently mentioned security questionnaire friction.
3. Offer 3 free packet reviews in exchange for objections and examples.
4. Add the most common missing answer templates to the bank.
5. Test a £199 setup sprint CTA before building multi-user features.
