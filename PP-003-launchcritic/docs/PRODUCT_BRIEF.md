# LaunchCritic Product Brief

## What it does
A self-serve MVP that turns messy user input into a practical output users can copy, send, or save.

## Who it is for
Small operators with urgent admin pain who are underserved by enterprise tools.

## Pricing strategy
- Free: one generated output.
- Starter: £21/month.
- Pro: higher-volume templates and saved history.

## MVP limits
Static, browser-only prototype. Email capture stores locally until wired to a backend.
