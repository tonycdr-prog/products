# Product Brief — LaunchLens

A founder validation wizard that turns an idea and buyer profile into pain-search terms, community plan, 7-day sprint and kill/continue evidence threshold.

## Audience
Indie founders, micro-SaaS builders and agencies validating demand before building.

## Pricing
Free single plan; £15/mo Sprint Kit; £49/mo Studio. Conservative target: 50 founders × £15 = £750 MRR.
