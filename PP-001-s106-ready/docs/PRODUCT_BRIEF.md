# Product brief — S106 Ready

S106 Ready helps UK first-time buyers track Section 106 discounted/resale approval blockers. It provides a status wizard, chase-plan generator, evidence log, and copy-paste email drafts.

Target user: buyers of discounted market/S106 homes and conveyancers handling multiple stalled approvals.

Pricing: free one-off checker; £15/mo buyer workspace; £99/mo conveyancer/team dashboard.

Caveat: not legal advice; workflow and communication support only.
