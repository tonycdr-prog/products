# Product Brief

QuoteComplete helps small businesses turn partial vendor replies into complete landed-cost requests and internal approval-ready estimates.

## Audience
SMB owners, office managers, makers, ecommerce operators buying custom goods.

## Pricing
Free calculator, £10/mo Buyer, £29/mo Ops.

## MVP scope
Calculator, missing-field checklist, vendor email generator. Future: quote comparison, PO export, saved vendors.
