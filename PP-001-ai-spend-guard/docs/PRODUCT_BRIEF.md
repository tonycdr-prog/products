# Product Brief — AI Spend Guard

## What it does
AI Spend Guard is a browser-only AI FinOps audit tool for small teams. Users enter AI tools, categories, monthly costs, paid seats, active users and renewal dates. The app calculates monthly AI burn, annual exposure, budget variance, unused-seat waste, duplicate-category consolidation opportunities, renewal warnings and a copyable finance/founder report.

## Target customer
Founders, fractional CFOs, ops leads and finance owners at startups or agencies using ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, research tools and model APIs without a formal AI procurement process.

## Pricing model
- Free Audit: browser-only ledger, risk report, CSV export.
- Team Guard: £15/month for saved reports, renewal reminders, budget history and Slack/email nudges.
- Finance Pack: £49/month for multi-team approvals, policy templates and board-ready monthly PDFs.

First commercial target: 40 teams on Team Guard = £600 MRR.

## Competitive advantage
- Local-first: no sensitive vendor spend leaves the browser in the MVP.
- Narrow wedge: focuses only on AI subscription/API sprawl, not generic SaaS management.
- Founder-friendly output: immediate copyable report with concrete cancellation/renewal actions.
- Low-friction upgrade path: reminders, recurring reports and policy exports are obvious paid features.

## Current MVP status
The static app now includes an editable spend ledger, sample data, paste import, CSV export, local save, duplicate-category detection, unused-seat waste estimates, renewal warnings, budget risk scoring, copy report and report download.
