# Marketing Plan — AI Spend Guard

## Landing page headline
Stop AI spend drift before finance does.

## 3 bullet benefits
- See total AI burn across subscriptions, seats and API usage in minutes.
- Find unused seats, duplicate tool categories and renewals before they waste cash.
- Copy a finance-ready report without uploading private spend data.

## CTA copy
Run the free AI spend audit

## Primary distribution channel
Founder/operator communities where AI tool sprawl is already being discussed: targeted Reddit/HN replies, founder Slack groups and LinkedIn posts from fractional CFO/operator accounts. Lead with the free local calculator and ask for anonymised screenshots of real AI stacks.

## First week launch plan
1. Publish static MVP and add lightweight analytics/waitlist capture.
2. Post “AI tool spend is becoming your second cloud bill” with a link to the calculator.
3. DM 10 founders/fractional CFOs with a concrete prompt: “Paste your AI stack; I’ll sanity-check the waste flags.”
4. Turn three anonymised audits into before/after mini case studies.
5. Add paid £15/month Team Guard checkout only after at least five users ask for reminders or recurring reports.
