# ShopReply Lite Marketing Plan

## First-week channels
- Shopify community forum
- r/shopify and r/smallbusiness where allowed
- App Store alternative pages targeting Gorgias/Zendesk queries
- Partner with Shopify theme freelancers
- Short Loom teardown of support inbox setup

## Launch tactics
1. Post a problem-first Reddit reply where allowed; no drive-by spam.
2. DM/interview 10 source-adjacent users with a free report.
3. Publish a before/after teardown showing the generated output.
4. Add Stripe Payment Link and Carrd/custom-domain deploy.
5. Track conversions in a simple CSV/Notion table.
