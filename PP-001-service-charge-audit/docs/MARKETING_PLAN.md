# ChargeCheck Marketing Plan

## Landing page headline
Find the service charge lines worth challenging.

## 3 bullet benefits
- Spot unexplained year-on-year increases before you pay blindly.
- Generate a clear managing-agent evidence request in minutes.
- Keep the audit local and private — no document upload required.

## CTA copy
Run a free audit → Join the £19 beta

## Primary distribution channel
Direct outreach and helpful replies in UK leasehold / RTM communities: HousingUK Reddit threads, leasehold Facebook groups and LinkedIn posts aimed at RTM directors.

## First-week launch actions
1. Deploy the static MVP to a public URL.
2. Add Tally/ConvertKit waitlist and Stripe payment link.
3. Offer 10 free manual reviews to leaseholders/RTM directors in return for feedback.
4. Publish one teardown: “How to question a 30% service charge increase without sounding like a crank.”
5. Turn common flagged categories into SEO pages: insurance increase, reserve fund demand, management fee increase, section 20 evidence.
