# ChargeCheck Product Brief

## What it does
ChargeCheck is a browser-based UK leasehold service-charge audit tool. Users enter last-year and current-year budget totals plus line items, then receive:

- Overall variance and risk level
- Line-by-line increase flags
- Evidence checklist for missing documents
- A copyable managing-agent challenge email
- Downloadable local text audit pack

The MVP is static and local-only: no documents are uploaded and no account is required.

## Target customer
1. Individual UK leaseholders facing confusing or sharply increased annual service charge demands.
2. RTM companies / residents associations comparing costs across a block.
3. Leasehold advisers and property consultants who need repeatable first-pass triage.

## Pricing model
- **Leaseholder:** £19 one-off audit/report
- **RTM / Residents group:** £99/month for shared tracker and multi-flat comparison
- **Adviser:** £249/month for batch audits and branded first-pass reports

## Competitive advantage
Most leaseholders do not know which evidence to request or how to phrase the first challenge. ChargeCheck turns raw budget differences into a structured evidence request in minutes, with UK-specific prompts around insurance, reserve funds, contractor invoices and section-20 paperwork. It is faster and cheaper than starting with a solicitor, while still avoiding legal-advice claims.

## Current MVP status
Working static prototype with calculator, checklist, generated letter, local waitlist capture and export. Next step is payment/waitlist wiring plus validation with 10 leaseholders or RTM directors.
