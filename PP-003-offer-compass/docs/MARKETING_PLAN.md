# Offer Compass — Marketing Plan

## Landing page headline
Make a property offer with evidence, not vibes.

## Benefits
- Turn three comparable sales into a clear UK offer band in five minutes.
- Check deposit, stamp duty and monthly repayment before committing emotionally.
- Copy a calm agent-ready note that explains your number without awkward spreadsheets.

## CTA copy
Build my offer report

## Primary distribution channel
Reddit and forum-led buyer communities: contribute helpful offer-analysis examples in UKHousing, FirstTimeBuyer and MoneySavingExpert-style discussions, then link to the free calculator when relevant. Focus on real buyer anxiety: “Is this asking price fair?” and “How much below asking can I offer?”

## Launch angle
Free browser calculator first, paid PDF/report export after trust is established. Position as a negotiation preparation tool, not a valuation service.

## First paid experiment
Offer a £10 manual-polished report: user submits target + comparable links, receives a clean PDF with evidence table, buyer-cost summary and negotiation script within 24 hours.
