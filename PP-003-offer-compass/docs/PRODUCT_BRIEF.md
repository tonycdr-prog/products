# Offer Compass — Product Brief

## Refined target customer
UK first-time buyers and home movers who have found a property they like but do not trust the asking-price anchor. The most urgent customer is a buyer preparing a same-day or next-day offer after a viewing, especially in cities where comparable sold prices are messy and estate agents create pressure.

Secondary buyers include mortgage brokers, buying agents and financially literate family members helping someone sanity-check an offer before negotiation.

## What it does
Offer Compass turns comparable property sales into an evidence-backed offer range. Users enter the target property, comparable sales, condition and market adjustments, deposit and mortgage assumptions, then receive:

- an adjusted evidence value and offer band;
- stamp duty, mortgage repayment and LTV estimates;
- a confidence score based on comparable count and adjustment risk;
- a copy-ready estate-agent note;
- local saved reports and print/PDF path.

## Pricing model
- **Free:** browser-only calculator, one-off report, local saves and copyable agent note.
- **Pro report — £49 one-time:** polished PDF-ready offer report review, comparable evidence checklist, negotiation note refinement and 48-hour turnaround while demand is validated manually.

Stripe checkout is represented by a placeholder CTA until payment is wired. The first paid version can be fulfilled manually to test willingness to pay before building accounts or automated data imports.

## Competitive advantage
Most property calculators stop at affordability or stamp duty. Offer Compass combines comparable evidence, £/sq ft adjustments, buyer-cost checks and negotiation wording in one lightweight workflow. It gives buyers a defensible anchor without claiming to be a formal valuation.

## Real distribution tactic
Publish a free “UK home offer evidence checklist” and post a worked example in **r/HousingUK**, **r/UKPersonalFinance** and **MoneySavingExpert House Buying forum**. The CTA is not “buy software”; it is “sense-check your offer before you call the agent.” Ask commenters to paste anonymised asking price, area and 2-3 comparable links, then use those replies to validate Pro report demand.

## Current MVP state
Self-contained static app with no external dependencies. Includes comparable rows, weighted £/sq ft calculations, condition/market adjustments, stamp-duty estimate, mortgage repayment estimate, confidence score, local saved reports, sample data, copyable agent note, print/PDF path, pricing, Stripe placeholder and email capture.

## Not advice disclaimer
Outputs are estimates for buyer preparation and negotiation. Production needs legal/financial disclaimer review, data freshness notes and a clearer audit trail for comparable sources.
