# Offer Compass — Product Brief

## What it does
Offer Compass helps UK homebuyers turn comparable property sales into an evidence-backed offer range. Users enter the target property, comparable sales, condition/urgency adjustments, deposit and mortgage assumptions, then receive an opening offer, affordability snapshot, estimated stamp duty and copy-ready agent note.

## Target customer
UK first-time buyers and home movers who are nervous about overpaying, especially in competitive or opaque local markets where agents push asking-price anchors.

## Pricing model
- £10 one-off polished offer report / PDF export.
- £19/month for saved reports, postcode benchmarks, sold-price import and agent-ready export history.
- Free lead magnet: browser-only calculator with local saved reports.

## Competitive advantage
Most calculators stop at affordability or stamp duty. Offer Compass combines comparable evidence, £/sq ft adjustments, buyer-cost checks and negotiation wording in one small workflow. It is lightweight enough to use before a viewing follow-up, and specific enough to justify an offer without sounding combative.

## Current MVP state
Self-contained static app with no external CDN dependencies. Includes editable comparable rows, weighted £/sq ft calculations, condition/market adjustments, stamp-duty estimate, mortgage repayment estimate, confidence score, local saved reports, sample data, copyable agent note and print/PDF path.

## Not advice disclaimer
Outputs are estimates for buyer preparation and negotiation. Production needs a legal/financial disclaimer, data freshness notes and a clearer audit trail for comparable sources.
