# Offer Compass — Launch Plan

## Week 1 posting plan

1. **r/HousingUK** — post a worked example of turning 3 sold comparables into a calm offer range. Ask for anonymised feedback from buyers currently viewing.
2. **r/UKPersonalFinance** — frame as a buyer-cost sanity check: stamp duty, repayment and evidence-backed offer logic before overcommitting.
3. **MoneySavingExpert House Buying, Renting & Selling forum** — share the free checklist and ask whether a £49 reviewed offer pack would be useful before negotiating.
4. **IndieHackers** — build-in-public post about validating a manual-first property negotiation microproduct.
5. **HN Show HN** — only after 3-5 real user examples are incorporated; position as a local-first calculator, not legal advice.

## First post template

**Title:** I built a free UK property offer sanity-checker so buyers stop negotiating from vibes

I’m testing a tiny browser-only tool for UK homebuyers.

You enter:
- the target property asking price and size;
- 2-5 comparable sold prices;
- rough condition / market adjustments;
- deposit, mortgage rate and buyer type.

It gives you:
- an evidence-backed offer band;
- estimated stamp duty and monthly repayment;
- a confidence score;
- a copy-ready note you can adapt before speaking to the estate agent.

No signup, no upload, everything runs locally in the browser:
https://tonycdr-prog.github.io/products/PP-003-offer-compass/

I’m especially looking for feedback from anyone currently making an offer or helping someone buy. Does this match how you reason about an offer? What would make it trustworthy enough to pay £49 for a reviewed report?

Not financial/legal advice — it’s a preparation tool to organise comparable evidence before negotiation.

## Cold outreach targets

1. **Mortgage broker** — LinkedIn. Ask if clients arrive with weak offer logic and whether a pre-offer evidence pack would reduce back-and-forth.
2. **First-time buyer adviser / property coach** — TikTok or Instagram. Offer a free branded example report for one anonymised follower scenario.
3. **Buying agent / relocation consultant** — LinkedIn. Pitch the free calculator as a lightweight pre-qualification worksheet and ask what a paid PDF must include.

## Success criteria for first week

- 20+ unique visits from forum posts.
- 5+ anonymised buyer scenarios submitted in comments or email.
- 2+ people say they would pay for a reviewed report or ask for one.
- At least one distribution partner agrees to review the output format.
