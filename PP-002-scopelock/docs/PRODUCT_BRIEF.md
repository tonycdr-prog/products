A freelancer utility that converts approved scope, new client requests, timing pressure and desired tone into a polite but firm change-order response with fee/time impact and acceptance language.

## Target buyer
Early adopter segment surfaced by the linked pain-point source.

Free generator · £6/mo saved templates and client audit trail
