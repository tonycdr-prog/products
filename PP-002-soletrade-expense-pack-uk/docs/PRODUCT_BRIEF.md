# SoleTrade Expense Pack UK

## What it does
A browser-only expense pack builder for new UK sole traders. Users enter expenses, choose VAT treatment, attach receipt status, map costs to practical categories, filter by tax year, export an accountant-ready CSV, and copy a handoff note.

## Target customer
UK sole traders in their first 1-3 years of trading, plus bookkeepers/accountants who need clients to submit cleaner expense evidence before self-assessment and MTD workflows.

## Pricing model
- Free: manual expense log, tax-year summary, CSV export.
- Solo: £15/month for receipt reminders, bank CSV import, saved monthly packs, and accountant handoff templates.
- Bookkeeper: £49/month for branded client portals and bulk chase lists.

## Competitive advantage
Lighter than Xero/QuickBooks, more guided than a spreadsheet, and focused specifically on the early-stage UK sole trader anxiety point: “what can I claim, what evidence do I need, and what do I send my accountant?” The MVP is self-contained and works without signup, which makes validation friction low.

## Current iteration
Improved from a thin logger into a usable validation MVP: local persistence, tax-year filtering, VAT split, category hints, receipt evidence checklist, row deletion, sample data, CSV export, and copyable accountant handoff note.
