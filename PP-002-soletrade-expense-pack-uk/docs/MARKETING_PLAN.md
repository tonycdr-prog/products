# Marketing Plan — SoleTrade Expense Pack UK

## Landing page headline
Turn a pile of receipts into a clean self-assessment expense pack.

## 3 benefits
- Log expenses with UK sole-trader category guidance and VAT split.
- Spot missing receipts and “ask accountant” rows before filing panic.
- Export a tidy CSV and handoff note your bookkeeper can use immediately.

## CTA copy
Build my free expense pack

## Distribution channel
Partner-led validation through UK bookkeepers/accountants: send the MVP to 10 small-practice bookkeepers and ask whether they would give this to messy sole-trader clients at £15/month or bundle it into onboarding.

## First validation script
“I built a tiny browser-only expense pack for new UK sole traders: categories, VAT split, receipt checklist, and accountant CSV. Would this reduce the messy-expense chasing you do with clients? If yes, what would it need before you’d recommend it?”
