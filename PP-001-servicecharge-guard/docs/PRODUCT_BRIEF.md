# Product Brief — ServiceCharge Guard

## What it does
ServiceCharge Guard is a browser-only UK leasehold service-charge checker. A leaseholder enters the current demand, last year’s demand, reserve fund, number of flats, payment deadline, evidence received, and suspicious line items. The tool calculates a risk score, flags high-variance or vague budget lines, generates an evidence checklist, drafts a managing-agent request letter, and lets the user copy, print, download, or save scans locally.

## Target customer
UK leaseholders, especially flat owners facing sudden annual service-charge increases, opaque reserve-fund demands, insurance jumps, concierge/staffing increases, or major-works provisions.

## Pricing model
- Free scan: risk score, checklist, and draft letter.
- £49 one-off Challenge Pack: exportable evidence bundle, follow-up templates, deadline tracker, and escalation guide.
- Optional £15/month Pro: saved audit history, reminders, multi-property tracking, and template library.

## Competitive advantage
Most leaseholders start with forum posts and fragmented advice. This tool converts their demand into a concrete action pack in minutes, with no signup, no data upload, and language designed for managing-agent evidence requests rather than generic legal content.

## Current MVP status
Static, self-contained, usable in-browser with vanilla JS. No external dependencies. Stores history and early-access email locally only.
