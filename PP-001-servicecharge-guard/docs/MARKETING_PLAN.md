# Marketing Plan — ServiceCharge Guard

## Landing page headline
Find weak spots in a service-charge demand before you pay.

## Three bullet benefits
- Spot sharp annual increases, vague major-works provisions, and missing evidence in under five minutes.
- Generate a plain-English evidence request letter to send to the managing agent.
- Keep a local scan history and export a report for advisers, residents’ groups, or your own records.

## CTA copy
Run a free service-charge scan → then unlock the £49 Challenge Pack when you need follow-up templates and an escalation checklist.

## One distribution channel
Start with helpful comments and mini case studies in UK leasehold / HousingUK communities: offer five free manual reviews, ask users to run the scanner, and collect before/after objections from managing agents.

## First validation sprint
1. Publish the static page.
2. Contact 10 leaseholders from recent service-charge complaint threads.
3. Offer a free review in exchange for feedback on whether the risk report and letter felt sendable.
4. Charge the first three users £49 for a manually assembled Challenge Pack before building accounts/payments.
