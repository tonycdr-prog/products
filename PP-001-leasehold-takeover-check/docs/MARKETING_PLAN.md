# Marketing Plan — BlockReady Check

## Landing page headline
Do not inherit a mystery block.

## 3 bullet benefits
- Score takeover risk in minutes before leaseholders accept unknown liabilities.
- Generate a clear missing-evidence request for accounts, insurance, contracts, arrears and safety files.
- Save or print a resident-group-ready report, then upgrade to a £29 reviewed triage pack.

## CTA copy
Primary: Try the checker
Paid: Request £29 report
Lead capture: Save result for follow-up

## First distribution channel
Resident association and leasehold problem communities: r/HousingUK leasehold threads, resident association Facebook groups, and direct messages to 10 resident-group chairs or RTM/RMC directors.

## Validation script
Offer: “If your block is being asked to take over management/freehold without full accounts, I built a 3-minute checklist that shows what evidence is missing. I’m looking for 5 resident groups to try the free checker; the paid reviewed report is £29 if useful.”

## First-week plan
1. Replace the mailto CTA with a real £29 Stripe Payment Link.
2. Run 10 manual validation touches to leaseholder/resident-group contacts.
3. Offer 5 free reviewed reports in exchange for testimonials and objections.
4. Track conversion: checker opens, saved leads, payment-link clicks, paid reports.
