# Product Brief — BlockReady Check

## What it does
BlockReady Check is a browser-based due-diligence checker for UK leaseholders, resident associations, RTM/RMC directors and advisers who are being asked to accept a freehold, RTM, RMC or managing-agent handover.

The current MVP lets a user enter block details, accounts supplied, liabilities, reserve fund, deadline pressure and evidence received. It calculates a RED/AMBER/GREEN risk score, generates a missing-evidence checklist, writes a copyable handover email, supports print/PDF, and stores email leads locally for CSV export.

## Target customer
- Leaseholders being pressured into a freehold or management-company takeover.
- Resident association chairs coordinating evidence requests.
- RMC/RTM directors who need a simple board-ready triage summary.
- Small solicitors, property accountants and managing agents doing intake before paid review.

## Pricing model
- Free: browser-based risk check, copyable email, print/PDF output.
- £29: paid triage report for one block, including reviewed checklist and email pack.
- £99: resident group pack with up to 3 checks, shared evidence tracker and meeting-ready summary.

## Competitive advantage
Most leasehold handover advice is generic, slow or trapped in paid professional calls. BlockReady turns an anxious resident-group problem into a structured intake flow with an immediate risk score and adviser-ready pack. The wedge is speed: a resident can see exactly what is missing before agreeing to inherit unknown arrears, disputes, insurance gaps or compliance obligations.
