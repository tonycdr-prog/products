# Product Brief — Leasehold Lens

## What it does
Leasehold Lens is a static, browser-only UK leasehold service-charge risk checker. Buyers or leaseholders enter service-charge figures, annual increases, reserve fund balance, planned major works, lease length, block size, budget headroom and management-pack notes. The tool generates a risk score, affordability stress test, plain-English memo, and solicitor/managing-agent questions. Cases can be saved locally, copied, or printed to PDF.

## Target customer
- UK flat buyers before exchange who need to spot service-charge and major-works risk before committing.
- Current leaseholders who need a structured challenge checklist.
- Buying agents/conveyancers who want a lightweight triage aid for early conversations.

## Pricing model
- Free: in-browser checker and one copy/print memo.
- £29 one-off: branded PDF pack with saved history, richer clause library and exportable solicitor question set.
- £99+ manual review queue: human-assisted pack triage for buyers, buying agents and leaseholder groups.

## Competitive advantage
Narrow UK/FM/compliance focus: it does not try to replace a solicitor. It gives buyers a fast pre-solicitor triage report using the exact service-charge, reserve fund, Section 20 and affordability questions that create anxiety in real purchase decisions. Static/local-first MVP also lowers trust friction during validation calls.
