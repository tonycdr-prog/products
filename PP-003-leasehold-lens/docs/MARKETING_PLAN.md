# Marketing Plan — Leasehold Lens

## Landing page headline
Find the flat that looks cheap but is about to get expensive.

## Three benefits
- Turn service-charge accounts, reserve funds and Section 20 worries into a clear risk band.
- Generate copy-ready solicitor questions before exchange.
- Stress-test monthly affordability if service charges keep rising.

## CTA copy
Run the free leasehold risk check — takes 3 minutes, no signup.

## Distribution channel
Start with UK housing/leasehold communities and first-time-buyer forums: post an educational checklist (“7 service-charge checks before exchange”) with the free checker as the practical companion. Capture anonymised feedback manually before adding payment.

## First paid validation
Offer a £29 “Buyer PDF Pack” to 10 users from community posts: branded report, solicitor questions and a 15-minute follow-up call. Track conversion, objections, and which missing documents cause drop-off.
