# TradeDebt Pack UK Marketing Plan

## Landing page headline
Build a debt-chase pack before the invoice gets ugly.

## Three benefits
- Calculate overdue balance, statutory-interest estimate, and late-payment compensation in one flow.
- Generate copy-ready seven-day chasers and letter-before-action drafts without staring at a blank page.
- Track evidence readiness before escalating to collections, solicitor, or small claim.

## CTA copy
Generate my free escalation pack

## Primary distribution channel
Permission-first outreach to UK trades bookkeepers and accountants: send 10 personalised notes offering to generate a free overdue-invoice pack for one client scenario, then ask whether £29/month for reusable packs and reminders would be worth it.

## First validation touches
1. Five UK trade bookkeepers/accountants on LinkedIn or local business groups.
2. Five trades owners in existing late-payment discussions where a useful answer is welcome.
3. Offer a free pack, not a generic demo.
4. Track replies, pack completions, and willingness-to-pay in a simple CSV/Notion table.

## Conversion hypothesis
If users copy/download the generated pack and ask for saved clients or reminders, the £29/month plan is credible. If they only want one-off letters, sell a £19 single-pack download before building accounts.
