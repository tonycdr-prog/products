# TradeDebt Pack UK Product Brief

## What it does
TradeDebt Pack UK helps UK trades and small firms turn overdue invoices into a structured escalation pack. The browser MVP now supports multiple invoices, outstanding balance calculation, estimated statutory interest, fixed compensation bands, risk scoring, copy/download output, locally saved packs, and an evidence readiness checklist.

## Target customer
- Sole traders and small trade businesses: electricians, plumbers, builders, decorators, maintenance firms.
- Bookkeepers/accountants who chase invoices for several small clients.
- Owner-operators who need a confident escalation workflow before paying a solicitor or debt collector.

## Pricing model
- Free: one browser-generated escalation pack.
- Starter: £29/month for unlimited packs, saved clients, reminders, branded PDF/text exports, and Stripe-backed account storage.
- Bookkeeper: £79/month for multi-client workspaces, CSV import/export, team access, and reusable templates.

## Competitive advantage
Most late-payment calculators stop at a number. TradeDebt Pack turns the number into the working pack: chaser email, letter-before-action draft, timeline, compensation estimate, and evidence checklist. The wedge is practical admin relief for trades, not generic legal tooling.

## MVP limits
Static, browser-only prototype. Data is stored in localStorage only. It does not verify current legal rates, send emails, collect payment, or provide legal advice.
