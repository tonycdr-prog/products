# StackSlope Product Brief

## What it does
StackSlope is a static, browser-only Supabase pricing-cliff calculator. A solo builder enters project count, managed plan cost, egress, storage, monthly users, and ops confidence. The tool estimates managed-vs-self-hosted monthly cost, assigns a risk score, explains the drivers, saves local scenarios, and produces a copyable migration/readiness report.

## Target customer
Solo founders, indie hackers, freelancers, and small agencies running multiple small apps on Supabase/Firebase-style managed BaaS tiers who worry about graduating from free/cheap plans into recurring infra cost.

## Pricing model
- Free public calculator for traffic and SEO capture.
- £10/month early-buyer plan for cloud-saved portfolios, weekly usage checks, launch-spike reminders, and export packs.
- £29/month agency tier for multiple client portfolios.
- £19 one-off migration checklist/report pack.

## Competitive advantage
- Narrowly focused on the pricing-cliff moment rather than generic cloud cost management.
- Gives plain-English recommendations, not just numbers.
- Can be deployed as a zero-backend static page immediately.
- Uses local saved scenarios now, making the transition to paid cloud sync obvious without blocking launch.

## Current MVP status
Usable in the browser today: calculator, risk explanation, local scenario saving/loading, report copy, and waitlist stub. No external dependencies.
