# Marketing plan — PetRentShield UK

## Landing page headline
Push back on pet rent without sounding like you’re starting a war.

## Three bullet benefits
- See the annual cost and risk signals behind a proposed pet rent or pet charge in under five minutes.
- Generate a calm, editable landlord/agent response letter in polite, firm, or negotiation tone.
- Build an evidence checklist before paying, signing a variation, or escalating for advice.

## CTA copy
Run the free pet rent checker.

## Primary distribution channel
SEO-led renter content: publish a practical “UK pet rent checker / Tenant Fees Act pet charge response letter” page and share the free checker in renter forums only where genuinely helpful and allowed by community rules.

## First-week launch plan
1. Deploy the static MVP and add privacy-friendly analytics.
2. Wire waitlist/payment intent buttons to Buttondown or ConvertKit.
3. Ask 10 renters/tenant advice volunteers to test three real wording examples.
4. Turn the best examples into anonymised landing-page proof.
5. Launch the £5 letter pack once wording has been reviewed and payment/download is wired.
