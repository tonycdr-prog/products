# PetRentShield UK

## What it does
PetRentShield UK is a static, browser-only checker for UK tenants who are asked to pay extra “pet rent”, a pet fee, or a pet-related tenancy variation. Users enter rent, deposit, proposed monthly charge, tenancy status, pet details, and the landlord/agent’s wording. The tool calculates annual impact, flags Tenant Fees Act-style risk signals, builds an evidence checklist, and generates an editable response letter in polite, firm, or negotiation tone.

## Target customer
Private renters in England and Wales who want to keep a pet, renew a tenancy with a pet, or challenge a vague recurring pet charge without immediately escalating into a formal dispute. Secondary buyers: tenant advice groups and renter communities that need a fast triage artifact.

## Pricing model
- Free checker: acquisition and SEO/forum traffic.
- £5 letter pack: paid download with response, follow-up chaser, and evidence bundle checklist.
- £9/month renters club: recurring templates for pet clauses, repairs, deposit disputes, rent increases, and agent communications.

## Competitive advantage
- Narrow, urgent wedge: one painful landlord/agent scenario rather than a generic legal-template library.
- Browser-only privacy: no account, no upload, no sensitive tenancy data leaving the page.
- Actionable output: risk score, money impact, checklist, timeline, and copy-ready letter in one flow.
- Conservative framing: asks for clarification and written basis rather than pretending to provide legal advice.
