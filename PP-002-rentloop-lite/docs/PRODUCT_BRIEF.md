# RentLoop Lite Product Brief

## What it does
Create tenant reminders, arrears follow-ups and maintenance checklists for small UK landlords.

## Who it is for
Bootstrapped UK-friendly SMEs/SaaS operators with a narrow, urgent workflow pain.

## Pricing strategy
Free single-use MVP, Starter at £19–49/mo, done-with-you setup at £199+.

## MVP scope
Self-contained HTML/CSS/JS, real input/output, email capture placeholder, SEO meta tags.
