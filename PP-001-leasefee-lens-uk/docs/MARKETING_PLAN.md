# Marketing Plan — LeaseFee Lens UK

## Landing page headline
Know what to ask before you challenge the service-charge bill.

## 3 benefits
- Turn a confusing demand into a clear risk score and priority level in four minutes.
- Generate a tailored managing-agent evidence request letter without sharing private data.
- Download a triage pack you can use before paying for advice or organising neighbours.

## CTA copy
Run the free service-charge check → Request the £29 triage pack if you want a manual review.

## Primary distribution channel
Leaseholder communities: carefully reply in relevant Reddit/HousingUK-style threads where people are already asking about service-charge increases, with a transparent free-checker link and no spammy claims.

## First validation offer
Offer the first 5 manual Service Charge Triage Packs at £29. Success is 2+ paid buyers or 5+ qualified calls from 50 targeted community visits/replies.

## Follow-up assets
- One SEO article: “What evidence can I ask for when my UK service charge increases?”
- One LinkedIn/X teardown thread showing anonymised before/after demand triage.
- Outreach note to leaseholder advisors/community admins asking for feedback on the checklist.
