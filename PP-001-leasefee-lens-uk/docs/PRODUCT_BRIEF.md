# LeaseFee Lens UK

## What it does
LeaseFee Lens UK is a browser-only triage tool for UK leaseholders facing confusing or sharply increased service-charge demands. It turns the demand details into:

- A 0-100 risk/priority score
- A structured issue log
- A tailored evidence request letter to the managing agent
- A downloadable triage pack text file
- Local saved cases for comparing multiple flats/demands

## Target customer
UK leaseholders and resident groups who are anxious about service-charge increases, reserve funds, communal heating, insurance jumps, major works, or unclear managing-agent paperwork.

## Pricing model
- Free checker: browser risk score, issue log, generated letter, and local export.
- £29 Service Charge Triage Pack: manual review of one demand with personalised evidence request and 48-hour next-step note.
- £99 resident group batch: consolidated issue log for up to 10 flats in one block.

## Competitive advantage
Most leaseholders are stuck between generic forum replies and expensive formal advice. LeaseFee Lens packages the first practical step: organise the facts, identify evidence gaps, and produce a clean letter before escalation. The prototype is privacy-safe because it runs entirely in the browser and requires no account.

## Current status
Iterated 2026-05-17 from a basic score-and-letter MVP into a more usable triage pack generator with local save, issue log, copy-letter, and pack download.
