# ChargeLens UK — Marketing Plan

## Landing page headline
Turn a scary service charge into an evidence-backed action plan.

## Three bullet benefits
- Score your service-charge increase and Section 20 risk in under 15 minutes.
- Get a tailored missing-document checklist before paying for legal help.
- Copy a clear managing-agent email asking for invoices, apportionment, reserve movements, and consultation evidence.

## CTA copy
Run the free checker → get a £29 sample report if your case looks worth challenging.

## Primary distribution channel
Leaseholder and resident-association communities: HousingUK-style Reddit threads, leaseholder Facebook groups, and direct outreach to 10 resident-group admins with a no-spam offer to run one anonymised sample charge through the tool.

## First validation sprint
1. Share the static URL with 10 leaseholders/resident-group contacts.
2. Offer a manual £29 sample report via Stripe payment link.
3. Ask three questions after use: “Did it find a useful question?”, “Would you send the email?”, “Would you pay £29 for a polished PDF pack?”
4. Convert repeated objections into next UI improvements.

## Follow-up content ideas
- “What to ask when your service charge jumps 30%.”
- “Section 20 notice checklist: dates and documents to verify.”
- “How to request service-charge invoices without sounding adversarial.”
