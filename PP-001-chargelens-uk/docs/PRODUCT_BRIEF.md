# ChargeLens UK — Product Brief

## What it does
ChargeLens UK is a self-contained service-charge and Section 20 triage tool for UK leaseholders and resident groups. Users enter annual charge figures, major works amounts, evidence quality, consultation status, and budget line items. The tool returns a review-priority score, flags suspicious increases/evidence gaps, builds an evidence checklist, drafts a managing-agent email, and can print/save/export the report locally.

## Target customer
- Individual leaseholders facing confusing annual service-charge demands.
- Resident associations / RTM directors coordinating several leaseholders.
- Property/FM consultants doing quick pre-audit triage before paid advice.

## Pricing model
- Free browser-based risk score and checklist.
- £29 one-off branded sample report/PDF for a single demand or Section 20 notice.
- Later: £19–£49/month resident-group workspace for saved cases, response tracking, shared evidence packs, and multi-property history.

## Competitive advantage
Most leaseholders either search forums or jump straight to expensive legal help. ChargeLens sits in the gap: fast, plain-English, UK-specific triage that turns messy demands into specific document requests. The current MVP is fully static and privacy-friendly, so users can test sensitive numbers without uploading documents.

## Not legal advice
ChargeLens is positioned as document preparation and triage, not regulated legal advice. Paid reports need clear disclaimers and escalation guidance for tribunal/solicitor scenarios.
