# PRODUCT_BRIEF — VATFlow Lite

## What it does
VATFlow Lite is an offline-first UK VAT invoice pre-flight tool. Freelancers and micro-businesses enter invoice details, line items, VAT rates, and payment dates, then get net/VAT/gross totals, sanity checks, a late-payment estimate, and a copyable client payment note.

## Refined target customer
- UK freelancers, consultants, and micro-agencies issuing 3–40 invoices/month who do not want to open a full accounting suite for every draft.
- VAT-registered sole traders and small limited companies who worry about missing VAT numbers, wrong rates, unclear payment references, or weak late-payment wording.
- Bookkeepers supporting small clients who need a lightweight pre-flight check before invoices are sent.

## Pricing model
- **Free:** VAT invoice builder, line-item calculator, sanity checks, late-payment estimate, local save, and text export.
- **Pro — £9/month:** reusable client templates, branded PDF export, due-date reminders, and bookkeeper-ready audit history.

## Competitive advantage
VATFlow Lite is narrower and faster than accounting software. It focuses on the anxiety moment before an invoice is sent: “are the VAT totals, terms, and payment note right?” It runs locally in the browser, needs no account, and can become paid through templates/export/reminders without blocking the free utility.

## Real distribution tactic
Publish a practical checklist called **“UK VAT invoice pre-flight checklist for freelancers”** on IndieHackers and r/freelanceUK, then offer VATFlow Lite as the free browser-based checker at the end. Follow up with cold outreach to UK freelance accountants/bookkeepers who regularly post invoice tips on LinkedIn.

## Current MVP status
Static, self-contained, and usable in-browser with vanilla JavaScript. Includes working calculator, local save/load, text report export, pricing, Stripe placeholder, and waitlist capture.
