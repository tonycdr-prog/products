3. **IndieHackers Product/Feedback** — Share the build as a tiny paid utility experiment for freelancers.

5. **LinkedIn** — Short post aimed at UK freelance accountants and bookkeepers.

## First post template

Title: `I built a free UK VAT invoice pre-flight checker for freelancers`

Body:

> I kept seeing freelancers ask the same invoice questions: “Have I calculated VAT correctly?”, “Did I include the right payment reference?”, and “What can I say if this becomes overdue?”
>
> So I built VATFlow Lite: a free, browser-only invoice pre-flight checker for UK freelancers and micro-businesses.
>
> It calculates net/VAT/gross totals, checks for common missing details, estimates late-payment interest/compensation, and generates a copyable payment note. No login, no upload, no data leaves the browser.
>
> I’m looking for feedback from people who send VAT invoices regularly: what would make this worth £9/month — reusable client templates, PDF export, due-date reminders, or something else?
>
> Link: https://tonycdr-prog.github.io/products/PP-001-vatflow-lite/

3. **Micro-agency operations manager** — LinkedIn. Ask whether reusable invoice templates plus due-date reminders would justify £9/month.
