# ChaseProof Trades Product Brief

## What it does
Calculate UK late-payment leverage and generate the next chase letter.

## Who it is for
Electricians, builders, creative agencies, consultants, and micro-SMEs.

## Problem
Small trades and agencies lose cash when invoices age without structured escalation.

## MVP
Single-page browser tool with structured inputs, generated action pack, .txt export, waitlist, pricing CTA, and no backend.

## Pricing strategy
£15/month for unlimited chases. Start with concierge review upsell only after validation.
