# Emergency Tax Fixer UK Product Brief

## Problem
A plain-English checker for UK employees: estimate if your tax code looks wrong, what evidence to gather, and exactly who to contact first.

## Target customer
People represented by PP-003 in `output/pain_points.json`.

## MVP
Static landing page with working wizard/generator, pricing section, and local demo waitlist capture.

## Pricing strategy
- Free checker/generator for traffic and trust.
- Starter paid report/payment-link product at £19–£29.
- Pro subscription at £79/mo where repeat use exists.

## Production next step
Wire email capture to Buttondown/ConvertKit and payment CTA to Stripe Payment Links.
