# Marketing Plan

## Channels
1. Reddit reply/post in the source community with a non-spammy checklist.
2. SEO page targeting exact pain keywords.
3. LinkedIn founder/freelancer/tax-payroll posts.
4. Direct outreach to people already discussing the pain.
5. Partner post with accountants/agencies/operators.

## First-week launch tactics
- Day 1: deploy static page and add analytics.
- Day 2: share a useful teardown/checklist, not a product pitch.
- Day 3: DM 10 source-thread-adjacent prospects asking for feedback.
- Day 4: add the top 3 objections to the page.
- Day 5: test paid report/payment-link CTA.
