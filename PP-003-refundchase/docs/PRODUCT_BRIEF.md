# RefundChase HMRC product brief

## What it does
RefundChase HMRC is a self-contained browser MVP that turns a messy UK evidence/complaint problem into a structured checklist, risk signal, and draft letter.

## Who it is for
UK consumers/leaseholders/taxpayers who have enough money at stake to pay for a faster, calmer first-pass pack, but are not ready to instruct a solicitor/accountant for basic organisation.

## Pricing strategy
- Free checker for SEO/social conversion.
- One-off pack at £10.
- £49 manual pro-review upsell while validating demand.

## MVP status
Static HTML/CSS/JS. No server. Waitlist currently saves locally and mailto CTA is placeholder; wire Stripe/ConvertKit before paid launch.
