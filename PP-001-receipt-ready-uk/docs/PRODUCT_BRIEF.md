# ReceiptReady UK Product Brief

## What it does
ReceiptReady UK turns pasted receipt text into a cleaned accountant handoff pack: editable expense rows, VAT split, evidence warnings, CSV export, JSON backup, HMRC-style checklist, and a copyable accountant email.

## Target customer
UK sole traders, micro-agencies, and owner-operators who collect receipt screenshots/PDFs but dread the pre-accountant cleanup before VAT returns or year-end accounts.

## Pricing model
- Free: browser-only single-user demo with CSV export.
- Solo: £15/month for saved cloud workspace, bulk upload, recurring supplier rules, monthly packs, and accountant sharing.
- Done-with-you wedge: £79 one-off receipt cleanup pack to validate willingness to pay before building accounts.

## Competitive advantage
Most bookkeeping tools assume the user is ready to run a full accounting system. ReceiptReady focuses narrowly on the painful “shoebox to accountant CSV” job, with privacy-first local processing, plain-English warnings, and a low-friction manual service path that can generate revenue before SaaS infrastructure exists.

## Current browser capabilities
- Paste one receipt at a time and auto-detect supplier/date/total/VAT.
- Edit every generated row inline.
- Track receipt image/PDF evidence.
- Calculate gross spend, VAT candidate, net spend, warning count.
- Export accountant CSV or JSON backup.
- Copy checklist and accountant email text.
- Save local workspace in browser localStorage.
