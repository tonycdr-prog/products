# ReceiptReady UK Marketing Plan

## Landing page headline
Receipts in. Accountant pack out.

## Three bullet benefits
- Paste messy receipt text and get editable VAT-split expense rows in seconds.
- Download an accountant-ready CSV with warnings for missing evidence, personal-card spend, and suspect VAT.
- Copy the HMRC checklist and accountant email so the monthly handoff is not a Sunday-night panic job.

## CTA copy
Start with one receipt — no account, no upload, no data leaving your browser.

## Primary distribution channel
Founder-led outreach to UK sole traders and bookkeepers on LinkedIn: share a short screen recording of “receipt text → accountant CSV” and ask one question: “Would this save you at least 30 minutes before your next accountant handoff?”

## First validation sprint
1. DM 30 UK freelancers/sole traders and 10 bookkeepers with the demo link/screenshot.
2. Offer a £79 done-with-you cleanup pack for the first 5 users.
3. Track objections: trust, accuracy, VAT confidence, accountant compatibility, and whether OCR/bulk upload is mandatory.
4. If 3 people pay or 10 join the waitlist, build cloud save + bulk import next.
