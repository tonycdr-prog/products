# Product brief — AI Spend Radar

AI Spend Radar is a local-first spend inventory for AI subscriptions and API costs. It helps developers/founders identify monthly totals, underused tools, owner gaps, and cancellation actions.

Target user: solo developers, small agencies, and early SaaS teams paying for ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Cursor, OpenAI API and similar tools.

Pricing: free local calculator; £15/mo saved/team version; £99/mo finance export and policy pack.
