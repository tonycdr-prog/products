# LedgerLite UK Product Brief

A low-friction UK solo-founder finance layer. Users paste monthly totals/CSV-like lines and answer VAT/business questions; it flags reconciliation gaps, VAT registration risk, director-loan notes and HMRC next actions without connecting bank APIs.

## Target buyer
Early adopter segment surfaced by the linked pain-point source.

## Pricing
Free CSV checker · £15/mo compliance reminders and exports

## MVP status
Static landing page plus working calculator/generator and local waitlist capture.
