# Product Brief

AST Clause Guard UK helps renters in England paste tenancy clauses and receive rule-based risk flags plus agent email copy.

## Audience
FTBs/renters signing ASTs, especially London renters moving quickly.

## Pricing
Free teaser, £10 one-off move-in pack, £9/mo renter club.

## MVP scope
Static checker for common clause patterns. Not legal advice; links to authoritative guidance should be added before public launch.
