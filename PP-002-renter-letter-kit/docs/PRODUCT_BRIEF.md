# Renter Letter Kit

## What it does
Generate calm, evidence-backed landlord letters. The MVP is a self-contained browser app with real calculations/generation and no backend dependency.

## Who it's for
UK renters facing deposits, repairs, rent increases or eviction notices.

## Pricing strategy
Free checker + £5/mo saved letters/export pack. Start with waitlist/manual fulfillment, then add Stripe checkout and account storage only after demand.
