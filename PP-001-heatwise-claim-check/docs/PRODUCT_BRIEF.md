# HeatWise Claim Check Product Brief

## What it does
HeatWise Claim Check helps UK leaseholders and resident groups assess whether communal heat-network charges are worth challenging. The static MVP now calculates a challenge-readiness score, estimates annual and multi-year exposure, identifies the main dispute angles, generates an evidence checklist, drafts a managing-agent evidence request letter, and saves up to eight cases in local browser storage.

## Target customer
Resident associations, leaseholders and block representatives facing opaque communal heating, heat-network, standing-charge, VAT, metering or service-charge evidence problems.

## Pricing model
- Free public checker for basic triage and letter generation.
- £29 one-off block triage for manual review of one dispute pack.
- £25/month resident group monitoring for ongoing evidence requests, deadlines and charge-change tracking.

## Competitive advantage
Most leaseholder tools are generic legal-information pages or expensive professional advice. HeatWise is narrow: it turns messy heat-network billing frustration into a concrete score, evidence list and first letter in minutes. The static version is deployable immediately and gives Tony a clear £29 validation offer without needing a backend first.

## Current MVP status
Complete static browser MVP: `index.html` runs without build steps or external dependencies. Data is local-only; no legal advice, payments, email delivery or document upload yet.
