# HeatWise Claim Check Marketing Plan

## Landing page headline
Turn a confusing communal heat bill into a challenge-ready evidence pack.

## Three benefits
- Get a heat-network dispute score in under five minutes.
- Generate the evidence request residents should send to the managing agent.
- Estimate potential recovery or avoided overcharge before paying for advice.

## CTA copy
Run the free claim check → then upgrade to a £29 block triage if the score is high.

## First distribution channel
Resident and leaseholder communities dealing with communal heating disputes: HousingUK, leaseholder Facebook groups, resident-association WhatsApp groups, and source-adjacent Reddit threads. Use the free checker as the hook, then offer the first 10 block triage reports for £29 in exchange for feedback/testimonials.

## First-week validation sequence
1. Deploy the static page and add a real Stripe £29 payment link.
2. Post a short “communal heating bill audit checklist” in 3 resident/leaseholder communities.
3. DM or email 10 resident associations with known heat-network complaints.
4. Offer to review one anonymised bill pack for £29 and publish the before/after evidence checklist.
5. Track: visits, checker completions, copied reports, payment-link clicks, paid triage conversions.
